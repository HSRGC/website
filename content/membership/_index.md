## MEMBERSHIP

According to Article 5 of the Society’s Constitution, the members of the Society are classified as follows (for specific details of each class of membership, please consult the [Constitution](pdf/katastatiko.pdf)):


a) Ordinary Members

b) Junior Members

c) Associate Members

d) Honorary Members



## JOINING THE SOCIETY

According to Article 6 of the Society’s Constitution, if a person wants to join the Society, they have to contact the Society by sending an email to relativity-gr@astro.auth.gr with subject “Joining the Society” and by providing their personal details as well as the names of two referees who can recommend their membership. The referees must be Ordinary Members and will be contacted directly by the Society.

Copy and paste the following in the bulk of your email:

---

Title (Mr, Ms, Mrs, Dr., Professor):

First Name:

Last Name:

Father’s Name:

Date of Birth:

Current Position (BSc/MSc/Phd student, Postdoc, Faculty):

Affiliation:

Mailing Address:

Phone:

Fax:

Email:

Webpage:

I accept Society communications via email: Yes/No

Name of Referee 1:

Name of Referee 2:

---

According to Article 7, requests for memberships are reviewed every 2 years by the Governing Board. The Governing Board reviews the applications and presents them to the General Assembly. It is the General Assembly that decides about the requested membership.

If one wants to pay/renew their membership in the HSRGC, the preferred method is via a [credit/debit card](https://payments.hsrgc.gr/).
