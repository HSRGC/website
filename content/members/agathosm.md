Lecturer in Gravitational Wave Physics and Data Analysis at QMUL and vice-president of HSRGC (2025-2027). 

Graduated at NTU Athens in the School for Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics; MSc in Theoretical Physics, Utrecht University; MSc in Arithmetic Geometry and Noncommutative Geometry, Utrecht University; PhD in Gravitational Physics, Nikhef - Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
Rubicon Fellow at DAMTP, University of Cambridge; Senior Postdoctoral Fellow at TPI FSU Jena; Senior Kavli Fellow at KICC/DAMTP, University of Cambridge.
