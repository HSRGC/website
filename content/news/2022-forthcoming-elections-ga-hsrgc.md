The [7th General Assembly](http://www.hsrgc.gr/pdf/2022.09.21_imerisia.pdf) of HSRGC will take place on Wednesday, September 21, 2022 at the offices of HSRGC at the Astronomical Observatory of the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, starting at 18:00, with remote participation.

Below we list the names of candidates that have been proposed (and have accepted their nomination) for the position of President and Members of the next Governing Board as well as for Auditors. The elections will take place on Wednesday, September 21, 2022, right after the 7th General Assembly of the Society.

As was also the practice during the 2018 and 2020 elections, we will not be sending ballots by post. An electronic online ballot [Ζευς](https://grnet.gr/2020/06/26/η-ψηφιακή-κάλπη-ζευσ-της-εδυτε-ορίζε/?fbclid=IwAR2PUmTzUHnhgaQFHbWTsonDANnIVFPVF-XSx5HxYkqFJE-FKZD-DZ2flRo) (that is provided by the greek government for such purposes) will be set up for absentee voters. All members in good standing will receive detailed instructions on how to vote at the beginning of September.

A member must have paid all past dues, as well as this year's dues of 15 euros, in order to be in good standing and eligible to vote. The **bank account of HSRGC** to pay the membership fees is:
