[ΝΕΒ-21 --- 1-4 September 2025, Corfu](https://indico.physics.auth.gr/event/18/)

The 21st conference in the series "Recent Developments in Gravity" (NEB) is co-organized by the Hellenic Society for Relativity, Gravitation and Cosmology (HSRGC) and the Research Laboratory "[Mathematical Physics and Computational Statistics](https://envi.ionio.gr/en/research/research-laboratories/mathematical-physics-statistics/)" of the [Ionian University](https://ionio.gr/en/). 

It will take place in Corfu, in September 1-4, 2025.
