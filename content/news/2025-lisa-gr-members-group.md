In May, 2025 ΛISA-GR was established as a Member Group of the LISA Consortium and aims to enhance collaboration between researchers focusing on milliHertz Gravitational Wave Astronomy. [Nikolaos Karnesis](members/karnesisn) has been elected as the leader of the Member Group and you can contact him for further details at: karnesis@auth.gr

[Kyriakos Destounis](members/destounisk) was elected in June 2025 as the representative of ΛISA-GR at the LISA Consortium Council. You can contact him for further consultation on the LISA Consortium Council discussions at: kyriakosdestounis@tecnico.ulisboa.pt
