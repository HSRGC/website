# HSRGC

Welcome to the official webpage of the Hellenic Society on Relativity, Gravitation and Cosmology (HSRGC).
HSRGC is the National Society of Greece on the scientific areas of Relativity, Gravitation and Cosmology, and a member of the International Society on General Relativity and Gravitation.

The Society aims at, strengthening and boosting research in relativity, gravitation and cosmology in Greece, making public aware of any development in relativity, gravitation and cosmology, and promoting the research work of its members.  
One of its main activities is the organization of the biennial meetings which initiated in Crete in 1984, under the title: *"Recent Developments in Gravity"* abbreviated as NEB from the initials of the Greek title: *"Νεώτερες Εξελίξεις στη Βαρύτητα"*.
